riskhouse · public contract screening

Every contract published in the Czech contract register (Registr smluv) is matched to the business register (ARES) and checked with six explainable rules. Only contracts with a warning sign go to a person; the rest are cleared automatically. A flag means flagged for manual review, not a finding of wrongdoing.

Contract values thin out sharply just above the 2 M CZK tender limit

Contracts for supplies and services, value stated excl. VAT, in 50,000 CZK bands. Above 2,000,000 CZK the law requires a public tender. Counts fall gradually with value, then drop by about a third right above the limit: consistent with contracts being sized to stay under it. It is a pattern to review, not proof.

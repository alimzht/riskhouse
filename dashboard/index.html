riskhouse

Every contract published in the Czech contract register (Registr smluv), checked against the business register (ARES) with six explainable rules. Contracts with a warning sign go to a person; the rest are cleared automatically.

Contract values thin out sharply just above the 2 million CZK tender limit

Supplies and services, value excl. VAT, counted in 50,000 CZK bands. Above 2,000,000 CZK the law requires a public tender. Counts fall gradually with value, then drop by about a third right above the limit, which is consistent with contracts being sized to stay under it. It is a pattern worth reviewing, not proof of anything.
